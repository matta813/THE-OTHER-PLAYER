# Game Design

Chapter 1 expands first contact into repeated reciprocal cooperation. The player gives the remote partner useful camera guidance, transfers a physical fuse, reroutes power and starts a local generator; the partner unlocks systems, returns a card and holds a remote contactor. Communication remains terse and practical. The chapter does not reveal the central deception.

Conditional adaptive beats are: light before entry after repeated door checks, a quicker returned card after repeated fast cooperation, and an airlock cycle initiated from a high-confidence approach prediction. High suspicion or low confidence keeps progress explicitly reactive. Earlier reliable replies can still produce one controlled silence and reassurance. The final airlock action has a mundane explanation ("thought you were ready") and is not dangerous.

Current task topology and cues are in [CHAPTER_01.md](CHAPTER_01.md). The target playtime is 30–45 minutes, but that duration and tension curve have not been validated with first-time players; the functional route may currently be shorter.

The facility visual language uses serviceable industrial modules, restrained green status lighting, physical power and door mechanisms, and room-specific mechanical sound layers. The player is given a brief hold for sealing the transfer hatch, starting the generator and requesting an airlock cycle. Toggle timed interactions is available for accessibility. This is a functional production pass, not a validated realism or tension benchmark.
