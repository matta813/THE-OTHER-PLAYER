# Art direction

Chapter 1 depicts a near-future institutional research facility that could be maintained by people. Architectural spans, fixture placement and equipment labels should explain function before mood. Painted concrete is structural, exposed galvanised metal supports services, darker coated steel protects equipment, and modest amber/red/green indicators communicate state. Wear accumulates at handles, thresholds and access panels; broad surfaces remain mostly maintained. The airlock uses heavier framing, gaskets and redundant interlocks.

The current Blender kit introduces a consistent manufactured edge radius, panel rhythm and restrained material palette. It is an initial authored geometry pass. Current screenshots still show overly broad flat surfaces, primitive surrounding architecture and sparse object density. Future art reviews should judge those images, not asset counts. The development capture positions are in `tests/visual_capture.gd`.

Use ~2.4 m wall modules, 3.2–3.4 m ceiling heights, 2.6 m airlock leaves and sub-1.2 m technical cabinets. The wider service spine is justified as equipment transfer clearance. Preserve walkable collision and stable facility IDs while replacing visuals.

A later visual review found several wall modules facing into the collision shell. They now face the room. The Security link terminal was moved clear of the CCTV sightline; a skid-mounted generator and airlock cycle selector replace two prominent box/cylinder props. Bench luminaires are placed over the Security and Generator work surfaces. These changes improve readability, but the broad airlock side walls and workstation composition still need authored detail.
