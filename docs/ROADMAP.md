# Roadmap

## Implemented in this slice

- Grounded configurable controller and consistent ray-based interaction prompts.
- Room A, framed door threshold, service corridor, Room B, sectional cladding and equipment, practical/emergency lighting, procedural surface materials, and fog.
- Contextual Other Player scheduling, structured habit memory, asymmetric trust, habit-backed predictions, scored anticipatory light assist, delayed acknowledgement/reassurance, and restrained terminal typing.
- Smoothed/confidence-aware behaviour model, expectation/suspicion tracking, adaptive debug overlay, v3 save migration, internal behaviour-profile format, deterministic tests.
- Positional audio emitters and asset slots; no authored sounds yet.

## Recommended next phase

Conduct hands-on playtests of the trust → anomaly → reassurance beat and tune its timing from observed player reactions. The slice has too few repeated situations to validate long-term habit learning; add a small number of repeatable, physical cooperation opportunities before broadening the story. Commission/integrate authored facility audio and footsteps, continue lighting/material QA, and improve accessibility/graphics settings. Do not add a large second level yet.
