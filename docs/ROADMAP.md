# Roadmap

## Implemented

- Grounded configurable controller and consistent ray-based interaction prompts.
- Room A, framed door threshold, service corridor, Room B, sectional cladding and equipment, practical/emergency lighting, procedural surface materials, and fog.
- Contextual Other Player scheduling, structured habit memory, asymmetric trust, habit-backed predictions, scored anticipatory light assist, delayed acknowledgement/reassurance, and restrained terminal typing.
- Smoothed/confidence-aware behaviour model, expectation/suspicion tracking, adaptive debug overlay, v4 save migration, separate chapter checkpoints, internal behaviour-profile format, deterministic tests.
- Connected Chapter 1 wing with physical CCTV comparison, item transfer/return, capacity-limited circuits and dependent devices, generator contactor task, communications panel and sequenced airlock.
- Positional procedural ambience, relay/motor cues and surface-dependent steps; no authored sound assets yet.

## Recommended next phase

The chapter's technical route is playable in automated tests, but it is not yet a verified 30–45 minute chapter. Conduct first-time human playtests, time tasks, find wayfinding failures and tune the trust → anomaly → reassurance curve. Replace prominent procedural housings with authored industrial assets and calibrated PBR details, integrate authored ambience/footsteps, improve terminal readability and accessibility, and run sustained GPU/performance profiling. Do not expand to Chapter 2 until those quality gates are met.
